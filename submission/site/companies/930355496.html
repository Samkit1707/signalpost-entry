Summary

What it is and does

VINTAGE CAPITAL AS (org. no. 930 355 496) is registered as a private limited company (AS) with main industry 68.320 Eiendomsforvaltning på oppdrag and business address c/o Industrifinans Forretningsførsel AS Oscars gate 30, 0352 OSLO. [ev-registry-1]

It was founded 1963-12-19, registered 1995-02-19. [ev-registry-3, ev-registry-4]

It has 1 registered sub-unit (Oslo). [ev-locations-2]

Registered roles include Daglig leder: Christian Fredrik Furuholmen; Styrets leder: Øystein Bøe; Styremedlem: Christian Fredrik Furuholmen. [ev-roles-1]
