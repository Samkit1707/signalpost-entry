What it is and does

NOVOOI AS (org. no. 827 340 162) is registered as a private limited company (AS) with main industry 74.110 Industri-, produkt- og motedesignvirksomhet and business address St. Halvards gate 33, 0192 OSLO. [ev-registry-1, ev-registry-2, ev-registry-3, ev-registry-4]
