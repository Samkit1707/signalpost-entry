Summary

What it is and does

NOVOOI AS (org. no. 827 340 162) is registered as a private limited company (AS) with main industry 74.110 Industri-, produkt- og motedesignvirksomhet and business address St. Halvards gate 33, 0192 OSLO. [ev-registry-1]

Its registered activity: “Selge varer og tjenester innen design og håndverk - og annet som naturlig sammenfaller med dette - samt deltakelse i andre selskaper, investeringer og nærliggende virksomhet.”. [ev-registry-10]

It was founded 2021-05-09, registered 2021-06-26. [ev-registry-3, ev-registry-4]

It has 1 registered sub-unit (Oslo). [ev-locations-2]

Its identity-verified website https://novooi.com/ describes it as “Curated Norwegian interior design shop and agency, for professionals and conscious consumers. Showroom in Oslo. Global shipping.”. [ev-website-1]
