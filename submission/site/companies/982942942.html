Summary

What it is and does

BRANDMAKER AS (org. no. 982 942 942) is registered as a private limited company (AS) with main industry 74.120 Grafisk design og visuell kommunikasjonsdesign and business address Rådhusgaten 2, 5014 BERGEN. [ev-registry-1, ev-registry-2, ev-registry-3, ev-registry-4]

Its registered activity: “Markedsføring, design, reklame, datakonsulent, webutvikling.”. [ev-registry-14]

It was founded 2000-12-28, registered 2001-02-07. [ev-registry-7, ev-registry-8]

It has 1 registered sub-unit (Bergen). [ev-locations-2]

Its identity-verified website https://brandmaker.no/ describes it as “Webdesign og Emballasjesdesign i Bergen. Brandmaker designer nettsider, 3D animasjoner, nettbutikk reklame og grafisk design.”. [ev-website-1]
